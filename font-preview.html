What is Romagnolo?

Romagnolo is a Gallo-Italian language from the northeastern part of the Emilia-Romagna region in Italy. It retains its own grammar and vocabulary distinct from standard Italian.

An Endangered Language

Unfortunately, Romagnolo is extremely at risk, classified as endangered by UNESCO. Younger generations have stopped speaking it as a native tongue, especially since the introduction of compulsory schooling in 1947.
